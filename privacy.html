2. Information the app stores

TradeBuddy may store information you enter, including:

  • Customer names, telephone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses.
  • Job descriptions, dates, times, prices and notes.
  • Quote details and statuses.
  • Invoice details, amounts, payment statuses and payment dates.
  • Business profile information and document preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device. TradeBuddy does not currently provide an account-based cloud synchronisation service.

3. AI Quick Add and Google Gemini

If you use AI Quick Add, the text you submit and any information included in the request may be sent to Google's Gemini service to interpret your instructions and generate structured records.

Do not include information you do not want sent to this service. Google's processing and data-handling practices are governed by its applicable terms and privacy information.

The exact information transmitted, and Google's applicable retention and processing arrangements, must be verified against the version of TradeBuddy being published.

4. Backups and exports

If the app offers backup, restore, CSV export or PDF export features, you control whether to create and where to store or share those files. Exported files may contain personal or business information. Keep them secure and only share them with people you trust.

TradeBuddy does not currently promise automatic cloud backups.

5. Sharing information

TradeBuddy does not operate its own account-based service or customer database in the cloud. Information may leave your device when you use AI Quick Add or deliberately share exported documents. Other processing by third-party libraries or Android services must be assessed against the app's actual configuration.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove it. Backups and exported files may remain wherever you saved or shared them.

7. Security

Your device's security helps protect information stored by the app. Keep your device secure and protect any backups or exported documents. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

TradeBuddy is designed for tradespeople and business use, not for children.

9. Your rights

If you are in the UK, you may have rights under applicable data protection law, including rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information for which we are responsible. The appropriate route depends on where the information is stored and whether it has been shared with a third-party service.
